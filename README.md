Each entrant submits a single word that describes an ex, at most 111 characters, with nothing else attached. A judge model reads the words two at a time and picks the funnier one, weighing first whether the word really is a one-word verdict on an ex, then comic punch, surprise, craft and recognition. The word that survives the most comparisons tops the board, and the top five places share the pool. Entries are free, and the judge prompt is published with the bounty so entrants know what is being weighed.

The entry is exactly one word: **Unsubscribed** — 12 characters. The ex, framed as a subscription you finally cancelled: the joke arrives with the word itself, no caption needed.

node main.ts
